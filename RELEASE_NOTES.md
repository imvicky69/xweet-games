### Full Store Description
```text
Xweet Games is a curated collection of tactile, minimal, and ad-free brain and physics games designed with a focus on editorial typography, fluid animations, and satisfying tactile feedback.

FEATURES:

1. WORDLE EXPEDITION
• Daily Wordle: One synchronized 5-letter secret word every day for players worldwide.
• Endless Streak Survival: Solve consecutive rounds where every word counts. Features a discrete hardness slider (Progressive Ramp, Easy/Casual, Standard, Expert) and flexible dictionary validation.
• Career Records: Track your total runs, solved words, daily streaks, and review your latest series logs.
• Give Up Option: Safely finish any run and reveal secret answers without losing your streak history.

2. TILT. (ARCHITECTURAL TOWER STACKING)
• Forge2D Physics Engine: Drop swinging architectural beams from an oscillating crane onto shifting foundations.
• Precision Landing Ratings: Score Perfect, Great, Good, or Glancing drops to build combos and unlock dynamic floor themes.
• Dynamic Tower Lean: Maintain structural balance before tilt angles exceed the critical threshold.

3. WORD LADDER (SHIFT)
• 1-Letter Transformation: Shift one letter at a time to morph the starting word into the target word using valid dictionary entries.
• Pinned Target Goal: The target word remains permanently pinned and visible as moves stack up.
• Daily Puzzle & Free Play: Play the curated daily shift or generate infinite puzzles with verified shortest paths.
• Animated Solution Player: Give up at any point to watch a smooth, step-by-step animated flow of the optimal transformation path.

CRAFTED WITH CARE:
• Clean tactile editorial design (Ivory, Carbon, Signal Lime).
• Pure vector icons and readable English typography—no cluttered emojis.
• 100% local, offline storage with zero invasive permissions or analytics tracking.
• Completely free of ads and paywalls.
```
